Genesis app (genesis/): licence

PicoDrive 2.04 (genesis/picodrive: the parts this build uses, unchanged) and everything made for it
here (genesis/port: the 68000 and Z80 cores in assembly and their generators, the line renderer, the
YM2612/PSG loops; genesis/console: the Leapster 2 app) are under PicoDrive's licence, the MAME
licence below: NON-COMMERCIAL use only. The built app (package/GENESIS) is distributed under it too.
As it asks, this repository includes the complete source of the modified build: build.sh generates
the remaining parts with the Python generators in genesis/port.

Components PicoDrive includes, under their own terms (see their file headers):
  * FAME 68000 emulation library, (c) 2005-2006 Oscar Orallo Pelaez, based on C68K by Stephane
    Dallongeville (cpu/fame; cpu/fame/fame.html: may be distributed freely, nothing may be charged
    for it, contact the author for shareware or commercial use). The credit it asks for:
    "FAME Motorola 68000 Emulation Library by Oscar Orallo (oscar@m68k.com)".
    genesis/port/k68 is an interpreter of its own that charges FAME's cycles; FAME itself is still
    linked for the instructions k68 leaves to it.
  * CZ80 Z80 emulator, (c) 2004-2005 Stephane Dallongeville (cpu/cz80; readme.txt: free for any
    non-commercial project, with credit).
  * YM2612 (sound/ym2612.c): from MAME's fm.c, (c) 1998 Tatsuyuki Satoh, 2001-2003 Jarek Burczynski,
    MAME development; the MAME licence.
  * SN76496 (sound/sn76496.c): MAME development; the MAME licence.
